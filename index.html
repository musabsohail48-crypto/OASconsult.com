TAX & BUSINESS GUIDELINES

Information That Helps You Stay Prepared

Good compliance begins with organized information. Use these general guidelines to prepare before starting a tax, corporate or financial matter.

01

Keep Your Records Organized

Maintain relevant invoices, receipts, bank information, business records, registration documents and financial information in an organized manner.

02

Understand Your Filing Responsibilities

Different individuals and businesses can have different registration, reporting and compliance requirements. Review your specific position before proceeding.

03

Do Not Ignore Official Notices

If you receive a tax or regulatory notice, review the deadline and requirements carefully and seek professional assistance where necessary.

04

Keep Business Information Updated

Changes in business activity, ownership, contact information, registration status or financial circumstances may require updates in relevant records.

PROFESSIONAL TIP

Prepare Before You File

Having complete and organized information can make professional review and compliance processes more efficient.
